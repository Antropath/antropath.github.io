I have now built a community of 27k+ LinkedIn members interested in down-to-earth, technical insights into AI.
People often ask me how I manage to keep up with the latest AI developments, next to a demanding consulting job at AWS, family etc.
The short answer is: I don't. I don't stay up to date on absolutely everything. It's also not necessary. Instead, I follow people and sources I trust and read their material.
Based on this first, filtered input, I decide where to dive deep.

[curAIted.dev](https://curaited.dev) is a website that automates part of this process, available for everyone. I built it as a weekend project together with my friends Robert Jindra and Andreas Parteli. The solution regularly (currently monthly, probably soon weekly) crawls high-quality sources of AI insights, summarizes the key insights and classifies them into topics.
Users can then decide how deep they want to go - a summary of the latest crawl to get an overview, summaries of developments in different topics (e.g. "tabular data ML" or "Reinforcement Learning"), summaries of articles or the original articles.

I hope you find it as usefule as I do. Feedback welcome! Now please go and check out [curAIted.dev](https://curaited.dev).
